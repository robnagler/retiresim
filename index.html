Retirement Simulator

Finds which accounts to spend from, and when to claim Social Security, so your money lasts through retirement.

Enter what you have and what you expect. Your finances are simulated year by year, including taxes and Medicare, to find a plan that does not run out -- and, among those, the one that leaves the most.

Export saves a copy of the settings below so you can re-import it. The program runs entirely in this browser window. It doesn't send anything to a server.

Not financial advice. The results are only as good as your assumptions, especially the investment return.
