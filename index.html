Retirement Simulator

Works out which accounts to spend from, and when to claim Social Security, to leave you the most money at the end of your life.

Fill in what you have and what you expect, and the simulator runs your finances year by year to the age you pick -- growing accounts, paying the mortgage, taking required minimum distributions, and computing federal, Colorado, capital gains, and Social Security taxes along with Medicare premiums and their income surcharge.

It then searches for the plan that leaves the largest total at the end: which order to draw the accounts down in, how much to take from each before the tax cost outweighs the benefit, and how long to wait before claiming Social Security.

Everything stays in your browser. Nothing is uploaded, and nothing is stored anywhere but this computer.

This is not financial advice. It is a model, and it is only as good as the assumptions you give it -- particularly the investment return, which no one can know in advance.
